Kasunduan at pahintulot

Please read all statements before agreeing.

Sa pamamagitan ng pagsusumite ng form na ito, aking pinatutunayan na ang lahat ng impormasyong ibinigay ko ay totoo, tama, at kumpleto ayon sa aking kaalaman.

Nauunawaan ko na ang pagsusumite ng surrender request ay hindi nangangahulugan ng awtomatikong pagtanggap ng aking alaga sa shelter. Ang bawat request ay dadaan muna sa pagsusuri ng shelter staff.

Nauunawaan ko rin na ang pagtanggap ng alaga ay nakadepende sa beripikasyon ng impormasyon, kalagayan ng alaga, at availability ng shelter space.

Kung maaprubahan ang aking request, kusang-loob kong isusuko ang aking alaga sa Angeles City Veterinary Office / shelter at nauunawaan kong ito ay maaaring magresulta sa permanenteng paglipat ng responsibilidad at pag-aalaga sa nasabing hayop.

By submitting this form, I certify that all information I provided is true, correct, and complete to the best of my knowledge.

I understand that submitting a surrender request does not mean my animal will be automatically accepted by the shelter. Each request must first be reviewed by shelter staff.

I also understand that acceptance depends on information verification, the animal's condition, and available shelter space.

If my request is approved, I voluntarily surrender my animal to the Angeles City Veterinary Office / shelter and understand that this may result in the permanent transfer of responsibility and care for the animal.
