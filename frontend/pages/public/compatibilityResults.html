About your results

Your compatibility scores are based on your quiz answers and our matching algorithm. These results are a guide to help you find the best match, but shelter approval is still required before any adoption can proceed.

How matching works

Lifestyle & activity

We compare your daily routine and activity level with each animal's needs.

Home environment

Your home setup and space are matched with what the animal needs to thrive.

Care preferences

From training style to grooming and time commitment, we factor in your preferences.

Recorded animal traits

Age, energy, temperament, and habits are considered to find the best match.
