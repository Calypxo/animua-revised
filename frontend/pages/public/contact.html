Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers before you contact us

These common questions may help you find what you need right away.

What is the process for adopting a pet through Animua?
You can start by browsing the available pets on the Find Furbabies page, reviewing their profiles, and selecting a pet you are interested in. After that, you may complete and submit the adoption form for evaluation.
What responsibilities should an adopter be prepared for before taking home a pet?
Adopters should be prepared to provide proper food, shelter, medical care, time, attention, and a safe environment. Pet adoption is a long-term responsibility that requires commitment and patience.
What should pet owners know before submitting an animal surrender request?
Owners should understand that surrendering an animal means permanently giving up ownership to the shelter. Since the shelter may have limited space, owners are encouraged to first explore safe rehoming options with trusted relatives, friends, or other responsible individuals.
How does animal surrender differ from temporary pet boarding or care?
Animal surrender is a permanent transfer of the pet to the shelter, while boarding or temporary care only involves short-term arrangements. The shelter’s surrender service is intended for cases where the owner can no longer keep the animal.
How can users contact the office for more information about adoption or surrender?
Users may contact the office through the provided email address or contact number for questions, clarifications, and further assistance regarding adoption or surrender concerns.
