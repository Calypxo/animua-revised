Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers before you contact us

These common questions may help you find what you need right away.

Where is the City Veterinary Office located?
The office is located at City Hall Building, Aniceto Gueco Street, Pulung Maragul, Angeles City, Pampanga.
What are the office hours?
The office is generally open Monday to Friday, 8:00 AM to 5:00 PM.
Can I ask adoption questions through the chatbot?
Yes. The chatbot can help with quick questions, guidance, and page navigation. For formal or detailed concerns, contacting the office directly is still better.
Can I submit surrender concerns here?
For surrender-related concerns, please review the Surrender page first. You may then contact the office or use the chatbot for guidance before proceeding.
What if I cannot visit the office right away?
You may use the chatbot first for general guidance, then call the office for more specific concerns that require confirmation.
