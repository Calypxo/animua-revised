ABOUT ANIMUA

Built around real shelter care.

ANIMUA connects adoption, compatibility matching, and surrender support with the daily work of the Angeles City Animal Shelter—making reliable information easier to access and act on.

Responsible adoption Compatibility-based matching Shelter-reviewed surrender requests

HOW ANIMUA WORKS

From Finding a Match to Responsible Adoption

ANIMUA guides users through discovering shelter animals, understanding compatibility, and submitting an application for shelter review.

01

Explore Furbabies

Browse animals available for adoption and review their personality, health information, behavior, and care requirements.

02

Check Compatibility

Complete the matching quiz to compare your lifestyle, home environment, experience, and preferences with available animals.

03

Submit an Application

Select an animal and complete the online adoption application using accurate information.

04

Shelter Review

The Angeles City Veterinary Office evaluates the application and provides instructions for the next steps. Compatibility results and submitted applications do not guarantee approval.
