ABOUT ANIMUA

Built for Adopters, Pet Owners, and the Community

ANIMUA provides an accessible public portal for people who want to adopt, report stray animals, or submit surrender requests. It supports animal welfare services by making shelter information easier to access, organize, and track.

Through digital records, compatibility matching, stray reporting, and surrender request support, ANIMUA helps connect the community with responsible shelter services.

Request for Animal Surrender

Before proceeding, please understand that Angeles City Veterinary Office operates with limited capacity. Surrendering an animal means a permanent transfer of ownership.

We strongly encourage owners to first explore rehoming through trusted relatives, friends, or personal networks before finalizing a request for shelter intake.
